## 🔊 Audio Synthesis Specifications

* **Master Sample Rate:** 48,000 Hz (48 kHz), 16-bit stereo.
* **Harmonic Bed:** 6-chord progression with subtle stereo detuning (+0.25 Hz left, -0.25 Hz right) and warm analog-style second harmonics.
* **Sub-Bass Braams:** 65 Hz exponential frequency decay down to 32 Hz with transient noise saturation at each scene transition.
* **Telemetry Ticks:** High-pass filtered 2,400 Hz pulses with alternating stereo panning at 120 BPM.
* **Loudness Normalization:** Calibrated to -14 LUFS integrated loudness with true peak capped at -1.0 dBTP.
